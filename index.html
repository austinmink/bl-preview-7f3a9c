Fire Style!

A Naruto-themed charity hip-hop album featuring the English cast — Yuri Lowenthal, Tara Platt, Ben Diskin, Tom Gibis, Henry Dittman, Cissy Jones, Neil Kaplan, Brian Donovan, and more.

Streaming proceeds support the NAACP Legal Defense Fund and Asian Americans Advancing Justice. Fire Style! 2 is reportedly in production.

About

Clay Dietz · Big Lugg

Houston, TX. Music shaped by trauma and mental-health honesty — written to reach people who struggle, not to polish the pain away.

Best known for Fire Style!, the charity collaboration with the Naruto English cast. Beyond the leaf: slabs, grit, and nights that don’t look away.
